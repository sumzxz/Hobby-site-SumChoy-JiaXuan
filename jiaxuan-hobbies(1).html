Techniques

Popular Types of Saltwater Fishing

Bottom Bait Fishing

Using heavy sinkers with fresh squid or live prawns anchored to the sea floor to target bottom dwellers like Snappers, Groupers, and Catfish.

Shore Jigging & Lure Casting

Casting metal jigs or hard lures from jetties into deep water to simulate fleeing baitfish, perfect for predatory fish like Trevally and Barramundi.

Night Squid Eging

Fishing during nighttime around illuminated jetty posts using specialized luminous squid jigs to catch fresh squid on high tides.

Did You Know?

Fun Facts About Marine Angling in SG

  • 1 Bedok Jetty is Singapore's longest saltwater fishing jetty, stretching over 250 meters directly into the East Singapore Strait!
  • 2 Barramundi (Kim Bak Lor) have razor-sharp gill plates that can easily slice through standard lines, requiring heavy fluorocarbon leader wires.
  • 3 Fresh squid caught right off the jetty is widely considered the ultimate bait for landing prized Golden Snapper (Ang Chor).
